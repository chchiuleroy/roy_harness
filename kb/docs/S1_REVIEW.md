## Limits stated after the fixes
1. Finding 6 is narrowed, not closed (see the row). An attacker with write access to the scanned folder and good timing could still win the window between the re-check and the read.
2. The recovery ghost check and the activation visibility check overlap for a changed profile or tombstone; they are tested separately by observing the rename, which only the earlier check prevents.
3. The `members_sha256` recomputation is defence in depth: the database triggers freeze commit members once a commit leaves staging, so tampering with members is caught by the unit, role and chunk comparisons first. A mutant removing the recomputation first **survived**; it is now killed by a test where assembly writes a wrong commitment into a self-consistent manifest (`rev_commitment_not_recomputed`). What it cannot catch is a commitment that matches wrong members: that needs both to be forged together.
4. 小o did not look at `cas.py`, `ids.py`, `profile.py`, `colls.py`, `doctor.py`, `writer.py`, `durable.py`, `home.py` (S0 files, reviewed in round S0), nor ran anything; nothing here was checked dynamically by 小o on Windows handles or locks.
